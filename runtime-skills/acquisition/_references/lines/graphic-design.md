# Line: Graphic Design

## What "good" looks like

- **Consistency across surfaces** — one logo variant, one palette and one type pairing
  across website, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and print.
- **Legibility** — logo readable at social-avatar size (128 px), title type sized for
  mobile.
- **Hierarchy** — one dominant element per artwork, not five competing focal points.
- **A brand system** — colours, type, spacing and voice notes written down and reused.
- **File hygiene** — SVG or high-DPI PNG for logo; no visible artefacts on primary
  surfaces.

## Common problems and how to recognise them from evidence

| Problem | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Inconsistent branding | Captured surfaces show 3+ mismatches (logo variants, palette, type). |
| Low-quality visuals | Captured logo shows visible pixellation or artefacts. |
| No brand system | Heuristic audit: no repeated palette or type across three surfaces. |
| New business | Places listing shows a recent open date, or a fresh domain. |

## Business impact in plain language

- **Recognisable brand = recognisable business.** Inconsistent visuals cost you the
  compound recognition every touchpoint builds.
- **Sharp visuals set the trust bar** for how careful you are about the product itself.
- **A launch pack removes the "what should our logo look like?" tax** every new
  business pays for months.

## Vocabulary — for non-technical stakeholders

Use: brand, palette, type, logo, style guide, launch pack, design system, campaign
asset.

Avoid without explanation: kerning, tracking, x-height, ligature, chromatic aberration,
vector, raster, DPI.

## Proof — mapping to portfolio `proofTags`

- Full identity work → `brand-system`.
- Fresh launch → `launch-pack`.
- Sharpening existing logo/type → `visual-quality`.
- Ongoing campaign asset support → `campaign`.
- International design-system work → `design-system`.
