# Line: Video Editing

## What "good" looks like

- **Cadence** — a posting rhythm the audience can rely on (weekly or bi-weekly), not
  bursts and long silences.
- **Captions** — every upload has burned-in or platform captions; the majority use hard
  captions for mute viewing.
- **Thumbnails** — three-part consistency: title style, colour system, subject framing.
  Readable at grid size.
- **Hooks** — the first 3–5 seconds set up a specific promise.
- **Retention** — chapter markers on anything > 5 minutes; trims on anything with dead
  air.

## Common problems and how to recognise them from evidence

| Problem | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Rough active creator | ≥ 3 uploads in the last 60 days with visible rough cuts. |
| No captions | `contentDetails.caption = false` on ≥ 50% of the last 20 videos. |
| Inconsistent thumbnails | Thumbnail grid shows 3+ style breaks in the last 12 uploads. |
| Gone quiet | Last upload > 45 days ago and the recent-gap trend is growing. |
| Long unedited uploads | ≥ 3 recent videos > 20 minutes with no chapter markers. |

## Business impact in plain language

- **Captions unlock mute viewing.** Most social viewing is silent; no captions = no
  reach.
- **Consistent thumbnails train the eye** to recognise your channel in a grid.
- **A steady rhythm compounds** — the algorithm and the audience both notice gaps.

## Vocabulary — for non-technical creators

Use: cadence, captions, thumbnails, hook, chapter markers, trim, retention, retainer.

Avoid without explanation: LUT, colour grading, waveform, keyframe, easing, sync
correction.

## Proof — mapping to portfolio `proofTags`

- Ongoing editing support → `retainer`.
- Caption work → `captions`.
- Thumbnail systems → `thumbnails`.
