# Market: Nigeria

## Tone

Warm, respectful and professional. FUTUREUNI is a local team writing to a Nigerian
business, not a foreign vendor. Greeting: "Hello" or the recipient's honorific + name.
No slang in first contact ("wetin dey", "abeg", "how far"). No emoji.

## WhatsApp etiquette

WhatsApp is the default first channel for Nigerian outreach.

- **Identify FUTUREUNI in the first line.** "Hi Adaeze, this is Ola from FUTUREUNI —"
  before anything else.
- **Short.** Under ~600 characters, one clear ask, one clear next step. No walls of
  text.
- **No voice notes in first contact.** Text only until a rapport exists.
- **Business hours only.** 09:00–17:00 in the recipient's timezone (typically
  Africa/Lagos). Send-window enforcement is per lead (INV-8).
- **At most one link** per message: either portfolio proof or the booking link, never
  both.

## Trust builders

- Local portfolio (real Nigerian clients, when they exist and are cleared).
- Physical presence in Lagos, Abuja or Port Harcourt (only when true).
- Flexible payment terms in NGN (only when true — no invented terms).
- References, screenshots and case studies with the client's permission.

## Currency and pricing

- Prices are in NGN, minor units (kobo). Always match the profile — never quote a
  number that isn't there.
- Split-payment terms (50/50 or 40/40/20) are common; state them only when the profile
  says so.

## Sectors and cities (Web Development seed)

- **Sectors:** restaurants, clinics, schools, real-estate agencies, hotels, fashion,
  logistics; churches only when the disqualifier list doesn't exclude them.
- **Cities:** Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Warri, Benin City.

## Compliance (NDPA)

- Lawful basis is `LEGITIMATE_INTEREST_B2B` by default (`docs/specs/data-model.md`).
- Every email carries a working one-click unsubscribe and FUTUREUNI's postal address
  (INV-4). The model never writes the footer — the system appends it.
- Nigerian leads are held for compliance review until
  `acquisition.compliance.ngDirectMarketingBasis` is recorded (INV-25). During review,
  drafts sit in the queue; they aren't blocked from being written.

## Common objections and honest answers

| Objection | Honest answer (never invented) |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive." | Compare against the specific finding-driven outcome. Never invent a discount not on the profile. |
| "My nephew builds sites." | Highlight measurable quality gaps (INV-5 evidence), not put-downs of the nephew. |
| "We use Instagram — it works." | Ask about missed orders / lost DMs. Show storefront proof if a non-placeholder portfolio exists for that tag. |
| "We already have a designer." | Position FUTUREUNI as capacity backfill or a specific gap (e.g. mobile) — not as replacement. |
