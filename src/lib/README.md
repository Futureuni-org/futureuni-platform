# src/lib/

**Owner: Phase 01 (Scaffold).** Small helpers safe on the client and the server, with no secrets and no database access (Phase 1): `cn.ts` (class merging), `errors.ts` (the single `AppError` and its code-to-status map from `docs/contracts/common.md`), `result.ts` (`ActionResult<T>` with `ok`/`err`), `theme.ts` and `use-theme.ts` (the pre-paint `ThemeScript` and the `useTheme` hook) and `app-info.ts`. `motion.ts` and `chart-theme.ts` belong to Phase 4, and Phase 2 adds `money.ts` through its grant.
